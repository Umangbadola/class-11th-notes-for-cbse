MEASUREMENT:

 Measurement is the process of comparing a physical quantity with a standard unit to express its magnitude.
  It provide a quantitative description of a physical phenomena.
  ex: measuring the length of a table with a ruler gives a value .
  KIlo>HECTO>DECO>GRAM>DECI>CENTI>MILI
 
 Unit of Measurement:
 A unit is a standard reference used to measure physical quantities.
 >It providea a way to express the magnitude of a quantity by comparing it with a fixed standard.
 ex: 5kg , 5 is magnitude; kg is unit of measurement.

 therefore any physical quantity can be expressed as nu , n is magnitude and u is unit.

UNIT
 Definiton

  ex: kg,metre,second

  They are scientists across the world to communicate results in a standardized way.

Quantities

 Measurable quantities
 Measurable quantities are physical properties that can be quantified.
  ex,lenght,mass.

 Non- Measurable quantified
 Non-Measurable quantities are abstract or subjective qualities that cannt be directly measured.
 ex: emotions,intelligence.

 Physical Quantities;
 A physical quantity is a property of a material or system that can be quantified.
 physical quantities represent aspects of the physical worlld to which we can assign numerical values.
 ex;
  Length,Mass,Time,Temperature and Force.

 physical quantity= NUmerical Value + unit
  Pq = nu

 Classification of Physical quantities:
 Classification based on direction.
  Scalar Quantities:
   These quantites have only magnitude(size or amount)
   They are fully described by a numerical value and a unit.
   Direction is not associated with scalar quantites.
   ex; Mass, Speed, Time, Temperature.

  Vector Quantites:
    These quantites have both magnitude and direction
    They are fully described by a numerical value,a unit and a direction.
    ex: Displacement, Velocity,Force, Acceleration.

 Classification based in dependency:
  Fundamental Quantities:
   These are quantities that are independent and not defined in terms of other physical quantities.
   They form the foundation upon which other quantites are built.
   In the International System of Units , there are seven base quantites.
   

   Mass                m  kilogram  kg 
   Length              l  metre     m
   Time                t  second    s
   Current             I  ampere    A
   Temperature         T  kelvin    K
   Amount of substance n  mole      mol
   luminous intensity  Lv candela   cd

  Derived quantites:
   These quantities are defined in terms of fundamental quantities or other derived quantities.
   They are obtained by combination fundamental quantites through multiplication,division or other mathematical operation.
   ex: Area = length * length
       volume = l * l * l 
       velocity = length/Time 

 Supplementary Quantites
  Supplementary quantites are quantites that are not categorized as fundamental or derived quantities but are essential for specific measurement
  These qualities are purely geometrical in nature and are used to measure angle.

  Type of Supplementary Quantites
   
   Plane angle : represent the angle between two intersecting lines or surface
    SI unit = Radian(rad)
    θ = arc lenth/radius

   Solid angle: represent the three dimensionall angle subtended by a surface at a point 
    SI unit = sterdian(sr)
     Ω = area of surface / radius^2

Dimension 
 The dimension of a physical quantity are the power to which the fundamental quantities(mass,length , time , etc)must be raised to represent that quantity

 Physical quantity                     dimension symbol
 Lenght                                        [L]
 Mass                                          [M]
 Time                                          [T]
 Electrical Current                            [A] or [I]
 Thermodynamic Temperature                     [K] or [θ]
 Amount of substance                           [mol] or [N]
 luminous intensity                            [cd]  or [J]
 

 DIMENSION FORMULA 
  Dimension formula represents a physical quantity in terms of fundamental quantities.
  general form :[MᵃLᵇTᶜ]
  ex: area = [M⁰L²T⁰ ]
